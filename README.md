# 📖 The Problem

Children with ADHD are often remembered for their challenges instead of their strengths.

Teachers genuinely want to support them, but face several barriers:

- Difficulty creating ADHD-friendly learning materials
- Limited time to personalize lessons
- Maintaining student attention and engagement
- Building confidence after repeated academic struggles
- Encouraging meaningful peer interactions
- Tracking emotional and social development alongside academics

As a result, many children gradually lose confidence, withdraw socially, and stop believing in their own potential.

**The problem isn't a lack of caring teachers—it's a lack of the right tools.**

---

# 💡 Our Vision

BELONG shifts the focus from **managing behaviour** to **building belonging**.

Instead of asking,

> **"How do we control the child?"**

BELONG asks,

> **"How can we help every child feel capable, valued, and connected?"**

---

# 🚀 What is BELONG?

BELONG is an **AI-powered teacher companion** that transforms ordinary classroom lessons into engaging, ADHD-friendly learning experiences while helping teachers nurture confidence, friendships, and inclusion.

Rather than replacing teachers, BELONG empowers them with AI-generated resources, classroom insights, and evidence-based teaching strategies.

---

# ✨ Core Features

## 📚 AI Inclusive Content Creator

Generate personalized classroom resources in seconds.

- Story-based lessons
- Comic-style explanations
- AI-generated illustrations
- Interactive quizzes
- Brain breaks
- Classroom games
- Audio narration
- Discussion prompts

Personalized using:

- Age
- Grade
- Interests
- Attention span
- Learning style

---

## 🌟 Confidence Builder

Help teachers recognize effort rather than mistakes.

Examples:

Instead of

> "Didn't complete the worksheet."

BELONG suggests

> "Worked independently for 10 minutes."

Daily confidence missions include:

- Lead today's activity
- Appreciate positive effort
- Give classroom responsibility
- Celebrate small wins

---

## 🤝 Friendship Builder

Encourages social inclusion through

- Peer buddy recommendations
- Ice-breaker activities
- Inclusive seating suggestions
- Cooperative learning tasks
- Team-building games

---

## ⭐ Strength Spotter

Teachers record classroom observations.

BELONG identifies strengths like

- Creativity
- Leadership
- Curiosity
- Storytelling
- Visual thinking
- Problem solving
- Helping others

Every child builds a **Strength Portfolio** instead of a behaviour record.

---

## 🎯 Smart Lesson Planner

BELONG recommends

- Brain breaks
- Interactive teaching moments
- Visual supports
- Hands-on activities
- Gamified learning
- Attention reset activities

---

## 📊 Social Inclusion Dashboard

Track classroom growth beyond academics.

Monitor:

- Participation
- Engagement
- Confidence
- Collaboration
- Emotional check-ins
- Peer interaction

No labels.

No diagnoses.

Just actionable classroom insights.

---

## 📝 Reflection Assistant

At the end of each day BELONG asks:

- Did every child participate?
- Who smiled today?
- Who looked isolated?
- Which strategy worked well?

Weekly AI reports help teachers continuously improve their classroom practices.
