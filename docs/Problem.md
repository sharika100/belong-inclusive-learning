# Problem Statement

## Why BELONG?

As mothers, educators, and researchers, we have witnessed a common challenge from three different perspectives.

As **mothers**, we understand the emotional journey of raising children who think, learn, and experience the world differently. Many parents struggle to balance learning and development while limiting excessive screen time. They constantly search for meaningful, engaging, and personalized learning experiences that keep children motivated without increasing dependency on digital entertainment.

As **teachers**, we see another side of the challenge. Every classroom includes children with different abilities, learning styles, attention spans, and emotional needs. While teachers genuinely want every child to succeed, they often lack practical tools to personalize lessons, maintain engagement, build confidence, and encourage meaningful peer interaction within the limited time available.

As **researchers**, we recognize that although significant advances have been made in Artificial Intelligence, very few solutions empower teachers to create truly inclusive classrooms where every child feels seen, valued, and capable.

BELONG was born at the intersection of these three experiences.

---

# The Classroom Reality

Children with ADHD and other neurodiverse learning profiles often experience:

- Difficulty maintaining attention during traditional lessons.
- Frequent correction rather than encouragement.
- Reduced confidence due to repeated academic struggles.
- Social anxiety and challenges in making friends.
- Exclusion from collaborative classroom activities.
- Being identified more by their challenges than by their strengths.

Many children gradually begin to believe they are "not good enough," affecting both their academic performance and emotional well-being.

---

# The Teacher's Challenge

Teachers strive to support every learner but face several practical limitations:

- Limited time to prepare personalized learning materials.
- Difficulty creating engaging, ADHD-friendly content.
- Lack of structured strategies for confidence building.
- Limited support for fostering inclusive peer interactions.
- Few tools to monitor social and emotional growth alongside academics.
- Heavy administrative workload, leaving less time for individualized attention.

Despite their dedication, teachers often lack intelligent tools that translate inclusive education principles into everyday classroom practice.

---

# The Parent's Perspective

Parents want learning experiences that are:

- Engaging without relying on excessive screen time.
- Personalized to their child's interests and attention span.
- Confidence-building rather than performance-driven.
- Supportive of emotional and social development.
- Reinforced consistently between home and school.

However, there is often a disconnect between classroom teaching and home support, making it difficult to provide a unified learning experience.

---

# The Gap

Current educational technologies primarily focus on:

- Academic performance
- Behaviour management
- Content delivery
- Assessment

Very few solutions help teachers intentionally build:

- Confidence
- Classroom belonging
- Positive peer relationships
- Inclusive participation
- Strength-based learning experiences

Technology should not simply help children complete lessons—it should help them believe they belong.

---

# Our Inspiration

BELONG was inspired by our shared experiences as a team of four faculty members from **Adi Shankara Institute of Engineering and Technology (ASIET)**.

We are not only educators and researchers exploring the potential of Artificial Intelligence in education—we are also mothers who understand the importance of creating learning environments where every child feels accepted, confident, and empowered.

Our vision extends beyond ADHD.

We believe that every classroom should celebrate **different ways of learning**, recognizing them not as disabilities, but as **diverse abilities**.

BELONG is our step toward empowering teachers with AI to make inclusive education practical, engaging, and achievable for every child.

---

> **BELONG is built on one simple belief:**
>
> **Every child deserves to feel confident. Every teacher deserves the tools to make that happen.**
