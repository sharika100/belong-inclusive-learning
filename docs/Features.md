# Features

# BELONG Core Modules

BELONG is built around seven intelligent AI modules that work together to help teachers create engaging, inclusive, and confidence-building classrooms for children with ADHD and diverse learning needs.

---

# 🧠 1. BELONG Create
## AI Inclusive Content Creator

Teachers spend significant time preparing engaging learning materials for students with different learning styles. BELONG Create simplifies this process by automatically generating personalized, ADHD-friendly educational content.

### Teacher Inputs

- Subject
- Grade
- Topic
- Learning Objectives
- Student Age
- Student Interests
- Preferred Learning Style

### AI Generates

- 📖 Story-based lessons
- 🎨 Comic-style explanations
- 🖼 AI-generated illustrations
- 🎧 Audio narration
- 🎮 Gamified activities
- 🧩 Interactive quizzes
- 🏃 Brain breaks
- 💬 Classroom discussion questions
- ✏️ Worksheets
- 📚 Revision activities

### Benefits

- Reduces lesson preparation time
- Improves classroom engagement
- Makes learning enjoyable
- Supports Universal Design for Learning (UDL)

---

# ⭐ 2. BELONG Inspire
## Confidence Builder

Children often receive more corrections than encouragement.

BELONG Inspire helps teachers intentionally build confidence through positive reinforcement.

### AI Suggestions

- Celebrate effort instead of perfection
- Recognize small achievements
- Assign meaningful classroom responsibilities
- Encourage participation
- Promote leadership opportunities

### Daily Confidence Missions

Examples:

- Lead today's activity
- Help another student
- Share one creative idea
- Present a drawing
- Organize classroom materials

### Outcome

Every child experiences success every day.

---

# 🤝 3. BELONG Connect
## Friendship Builder

Social inclusion is just as important as academic success.

BELONG Connect helps teachers foster meaningful peer relationships.

### AI Recommendations

- Peer buddy matching
- Cooperative learning groups
- Ice-breaker games
- Inclusive classroom activities
- Collaborative projects
- Rotating seating plans

### Goal

Every child has opportunities to participate, communicate, and build friendships.

---

# 🌟 4. BELONG Discover
## Strength Spotter

Instead of recording only difficulties, BELONG helps teachers discover each child's unique strengths.

### Teachers Record

- Classroom observations
- Interests
- Successful activities
- Positive behaviour
- Creativity
- Leadership moments

### AI Identifies

- Creativity
- Curiosity
- Storytelling
- Problem-solving
- Leadership
- Artistic ability
- Visual thinking
- Collaboration

### Output

A personalized **Strength Portfolio** that grows throughout the academic year.

---

# 📚 5. BELONG Plan
## Smart Lesson Planner

BELONG analyzes lesson plans and recommends ways to improve student engagement.

### AI Suggestions

- Interactive teaching moments
- Brain breaks
- Visual aids
- Group activities
- Hands-on learning
- Movement opportunities
- Question prompts

### Purpose

Transform traditional lectures into engaging classroom experiences.

### Dashboard Tracks

- Confidence growth

- Weekly classroom summary
- Participation trends
- Inclusion insights
- Teacher recommendations

---

# 📝 7. BELONG Reflect
## Reflection Assistant

Reflection helps teachers continuously improve their classroom practices.

At the end of each day BELONG asks reflective questions.

Examples

- Did every child participate today?
- Who appeared most engaged?
- Who seemed left out?
- Which activity worked best?
- Which student showed confidence today?

### AI Generates

- Weekly Reflection Report
- Inclusive teaching recommendations
- Suggested classroom improvements
- Personalized teaching tips
