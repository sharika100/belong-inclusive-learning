# User Flow

# BELONG User Journey

BELONG is designed to seamlessly integrate into a teacher's daily classroom routine.

Instead of requiring teachers to learn complex software, BELONG acts as an intelligent teaching companion that supports lesson planning, classroom engagement, and student inclusion.

---

# Primary User

👩‍🏫 Teacher

The teacher is the primary user of the BELONG platform.

Teachers use BELONG to:

- Generate inclusive learning materials
- Personalize classroom activities
- Build student confidence
- Improve social interaction
- Monitor classroom engagement
- Reflect on teaching practices

# Detailed Workflow

## Step 1 — Teacher Login

The teacher securely logs into BELONG.

The dashboard displays:

- Today's lessons
- Student profiles
- Previous lesson history
- AI recommendations
- Weekly classroom insights

---

## Step 2 — Create a Lesson

The teacher selects

- Grade
- Subject
- Topic
- Learning Objectives

Example

Subject:
Science

Topic:
Water Cycle

Grade:
Class 5

---

## Step 3 — Add Classroom Context

Teachers can optionally provide:

- Student interests
- Classroom observations
- Attention challenges
- Learning preferences

Example

Rahul enjoys space.

Ananya likes stories.

Class becomes distracted after 20 minutes.

---

## Step 4 — AI Personalization

BELONG analyzes

- ADHD learning principles
- Universal Design for Learning (UDL)
- Student interests
- Previous classroom observations
- Learning objectives

The AI determines the best learning approach.

---

## Step 5 — AI Content Generation

Within seconds BELONG generates

📖 Story Lesson

🎨 Comic Version

🎧 Audio Narration

🧩 Interactive Quiz

🏃 Brain Break

🎮 Classroom Game

💬 Discussion Questions

⭐ Confidence Mission

🤝 Friendship Activity

📄 Teacher Tips

---

## Step 6 — Teacher Review

Teachers remain in complete control.

They can

- Edit content
- Regenerate activities
- Add custom notes
- Remove sections
- Save lesson templates

BELONG supports teachers—it never replaces them.

---

## Step 7 — Classroom Delivery

The teacher uses the generated content during class.

Students participate through

- Storytelling
- Group activities
- Games
- Discussions
- Movement breaks
- Interactive learning

The classroom becomes more engaging and inclusive.

---

## Step 8 — Classroom Observation

After class, the teacher records observations.

Examples

- Students enjoyed storytelling.
- Rahul completed the quiz independently.
- Brain break improved attention.
- Group activity increased participation.

Recording observations takes less than two minutes.

---

## Step 9 — AI Reflection

BELONG analyzes classroom observations.

It generates

Weekly Reflection Report

Including

- Classroom engagement
- Student participation
- Confidence growth
- Friendship development
- Suggested teaching improvements

# Key Design Principles

BELONG follows a simple philosophy throughout the user journey:

✅ AI assists—not replaces—the teacher.

✅ Teachers remain in complete control of all generated content.

✅ Every recommendation is editable and transparent.

✅ Every lesson is personalized to support diverse learning needs.

✅ Every classroom interaction contributes to continuous improvement.

---

# Final Outcome

By following this workflow, BELONG enables teachers to:

- Reduce lesson preparation time.
- Create engaging and personalized learning experiences.
- Build student confidence.
- Foster positive peer relationships.
- Encourage inclusive classroom participation.
- Continuously improve teaching through AI-powered insights.

Ultimately, BELONG helps transform classrooms into environments where **every child feels seen, supported, and valued.**
